# I'm Joey Rocha,
the owner of J.R. Home Solutions and for me, this isn't just a business, it's a calling. I've always believed that your home should be more than just the place you live &mdash; it should be the space that inspires you, reflects who you are, and brings your family together every single day.

What stated with a few tools, a dream, and a deep respect for craftsmanship has grown into something far greater. A company built on integrity, hard work, and passion. At J.R. Home Solutions, we specialize in custom kitchens and spa-like bathrooms that blend luxury with comfort, beauty with function. Every detail, from the cabinetry to the tile lines are handled with care as I treat every project like it's being crafted for my own home.

I am proud to work with homeowners across Butler County Ohio, and the surrounding areas. Helping homeowners turn their visions into spaces that don't only look incredible, but feel like home. 

My goal is a simple one: To create places where families make memories, where mornings feel fresh again, and where you can truly say: "I love where I live!"
